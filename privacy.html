🐯 Maolio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Maolio is a personal, browser-based vocabulary trainer for learning Mandarin Chinese. It is a static web app with no backend server, no user accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains exactly where your data goes.

Data stored on your device

Your vocabulary sheets are saved in your browser's localStorage on your own device, together with in-app backup copies. The optional file backup writes a maolio-backup.json file to a location you choose (e.g. your Downloads folder). None of this data is ever sent to the developer or to any server.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you connect Google Drive sync, Maolio stores a backup of your vocabulary in a hidden application data folder in your own Google Drive. The app can only read and write files it created there; it cannot see any of your other Drive files. The Google access token is held only in the browser's memory during your session and is not stored anywhere. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account permissions and delete the synced data by disconnecting the sync in the app.

Third-party network requests

Only two features send data over the internet, and only the word you are translating:

  • Auto-translate (译): the word or phrase you typed is sent to the Google Translate public endpoint, with MyMemory as a fallback, to fetch the translation.
  • Pronunciation (▶): uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis, which happens locally on your device.

No other personal data is transmitted.

Data deletion

Clearing the browser's site data for this site permanently removes your vocabulary from that device. Restoring from, or deleting, the backup file is under your control. Drive-synced data disappears when you delete the app's folder from your Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests regarding your data can be raised via the GitHub issue tracker.
